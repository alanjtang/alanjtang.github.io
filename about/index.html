ALAN TANG / ABOUT ME

Hello, I’m Alan.

A student of history, strategy, and the institutions that shape public life.

University of Virginia · History & Foreign Affairs

01History & strategy

I’m a first-year student at the University of Virginia with interests in military history, U.S. grand strategy, and foreign policy. I’m especially interested in how the United States uses military, diplomatic, and economic power—and how the choices available to policymakers are shaped by history.

Much of my attention is on U.S.–China strategic competition. I study the connections between national security and economic activity: critical minerals, strategic supply chains, industrial capacity, and access to technology. These interests bring together questions about diplomacy and military power with the practical realities of what countries can produce, obtain, and sustain.

My historical research has also examined Maoist suppression and control of religion during the Cold War. As a student researcher working with Professor Xiaofei Kang at George Washington University, I explored the relationship between state policy and religious practice in the People’s Republic of China.

02Research & writing

At UVA, I serve as Publication Chair and an executive board member of the Alexander Hamilton Society chapter. My work includes writing about U.S. national security and foreign policy, coordinating student contributions, setting editorial priorities, and helping organize chapter programming.

I’m also building Polarity Group, a student-led research initiative focused on U.S.–China strategic competition. Our work examines industrial capacity, strategic resources, and technology dependencies. One project maps the companies and facilities behind rare-earth permanent magnets, with attention to production status, supply chains, and alternatives to Chinese production.

I want that research to be useful as well as interesting: clear writing, transparent evidence, and visual analysis that helps readers understand where strategic dependencies actually matter. My essays and research are collected on the Publications page.

03Law & service

My experience also extends to legal research and public service. During an internship in the Domestic Relations Branch of the D.C. Superior Court, I prepared case summaries, worked with court documents, and observed proceedings. At UVA, I review scholarship and sources as an editor for the Virginia Undergraduate Law Review and conduct legal research through Student Council’s Student Legal Services Committee.

Education has been another important part of my work. At Tutoring for a Good Cause, I taught history to K–8 students, led educational programming, and later served on the organization’s governing committee. Those roles involved both teaching and the coordination needed to support a broader volunteer community.

04Beyond the work

Outside research and writing, I study accelerated Chinese, run, and lift. I follow football and am always up for a Messi–Ronaldo debate. I also play Clash Royale: I am a Grand Champion and have beaten top players. I am involved with UVA Clash Royale—an entirely different setting for thinking about strategy.
